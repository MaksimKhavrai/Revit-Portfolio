# Revit Portfolio

A portfolio of Autodesk Revit projects demonstrating my experience with BIM modeling, construction documentation, and building design.

## About This Portfolio

This repository contains Revit projects completed as part of my academic studies and personal practice. The projects demonstrate the development of building models through different stages, from initial levels and structural grids to architectural and structural elements.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Autodesk Revit
- BIM Modeling
- Levels and Structural Grids
- Walls and Pilasters
- Foundations and Footings
- Floors and Structural Elements
- Construction Documentation
- 3D Building Modeling

## Portfolio Structure

### 01 - Academic Projects

Projects completed during my studies in Applied Construction Management.

Each project is organized into separate stages to show the development of the Revit model step by step.

Revit model files (`.rvt`) and PNG previews are provided where applicable.
